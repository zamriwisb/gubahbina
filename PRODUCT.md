## Stack

Astro — chosen by the user for static output, minimal client JS, strong Core Web Vitals, and per-page SEO control across a multi-page BM site. Multi-page structure confirmed: Home, Perkhidmatan, Kelebihan Gabion Wall, Projek, FAQ, Hubungi Kami.

## Users

Malaysian property and land owners facing a physical failure on their site — an eroding riverbank, a slipping slope, a retaining wall that is cracking, or ground that floods and washes out. They are usually not engineers. They arrive worried, often after visible damage or a near-miss, wanting to know three things fast: is gabion the right fix, roughly what does it cost, and can someone come look at my site.

Confirmed audience segments (from brief):

- Pemilik rumah with hakisan or cerun problems on their property.
- Pemilik tanah dan kebun protecting agricultural or vacant land.
- Pemaju perumahan and small contractors needing a subcontracted specialist.
- Kontraktor awam and construction companies.
- Anyone responsible for tebing, longkang, sungai, or cerun protection.

The dominant device is mobile. The dominant next action is WhatsApp, not a form.

## Product Purpose

A lead-generation website for a gabion wall supply-and-installation specialist. Success is a WhatsApp inquiry or site-inspection request from a qualified visitor. Secondary success is the visitor understanding what a gabion wall is and why it suits their problem, so the conversation that follows starts informed.

The site is not a catalog and not a corporate brochure. Every section exists to move someone from "my tebing is collapsing" to "I have messaged the specialist."

## Positioning

**Pakar Memasang Gabion Wall untuk Tebing, Cerun & Kawalan Hakisan.**

Specialist, not general contractor. The differentiator is focus: gabion wall installation as the core discipline rather than one line item among many building services. Solutions are proposed based on actual site conditions, and the entry point to that conversation is deliberately low-friction — WhatsApp, not a quotation portal.

## Operating Context

- Visitors arrive from BM organic search on high-intent problem keywords ("kawalan hakisan tebing", "pasang gabion wall", "cegah tanah runtuh") and from Facebook.
- Most browsing happens on a phone, frequently on mobile data, sometimes while standing at the damaged site.
- The decision often involves showing the page to a spouse, business partner, or engineer before contacting.
- The sales process runs: WhatsApp message → informal problem description, often with the customer's own photos → site inspection (semakan tapak) → sebut harga → installation.
- Customers commonly cannot name their problem in technical terms. They describe symptoms: "tanah jatuh", "air masuk", "tembok retak".

## Brand Commitments

- Core positioning line, fixed: **Pakar Memasang Gabion Wall untuk Tebing, Cerun & Kawalan Hakisan**.
- Voice: direct, practical, problem-solution led, locally Malaysian, trustworthy without exaggeration. No hype, no false urgency.
- Explicitly rejected by the client brief: generic corporate/SaaS style, tech-startup aesthetics, futuristic or glowing UI, stock imagery of business people in suits, over-designed animation.
- Explicitly requested: rugged, grounded, engineering-led character reflecting real site work.
- No logo supplied yet.

### Brand colour — client-supplied, binding

**#e14024.** Supplied by the client and outranks any palette derived from the brief's wording. The build originally carried an olive second ground taken from the brief's "earth tones … olive" line; olive was retired entirely when the real brand colour arrived, because red-orange and olive-green are near complementaries and competed for the same role. Neutrals stay graphite and mineral stone. See DESIGN.md for the contrast-stepped ramp — #e14024 itself is 4.24:1 with white and cannot carry small text.

### Visual approach — superseded, then revised

The user was first offered a set of distinctive visual worlds and **chose the category standard**: the conventional Malaysian contractor arrangement, executed at full craft.

**That preference has since been revised by the user.** On reviewing the built result they judged it "separated by bulky sections" and asked for a fluid, more interactive page. The homepage was then **fully re-composed** away from the stacked-band canon: fewer, longer colour runs; elements overlapping every field change; deliberately varied density; asymmetric offset grids in place of the centred column; full-bleed imagery breaking the wrap.

Interior pages retain more of the conventional arrangement. Do not "restore" the canon on the homepage — the change was requested after seeing the alternative.

### Constraints that still bind regardless of direction

- The brief's ban on over-designed animation stands. **Purposeful motion only** — user-confirmed: motion that explains or responds to input (the cross-section filling, annotations opening, hover and focus). No entrance animation fired merely because an element scrolled into view, and no scroll-jacking.
- The audience is on phones, frequently on mobile data, often outdoors in glare. Interactivity must degrade: keyboard reachable, touch targets comfortable, and useful with JavaScript unavailable.
- Interactive elements earn their place by serving the sale or the explanation, never as decoration. User selected the interactive cross-section; a before/after slider and a symptom→solution selector were offered and declined.

**Craft bar: premium Malaysian SME service brands** — the better-built local solar, roofing, interior and renovation contractor sites. The build must reach that finish level: real photography treatment, confident typographic hierarchy, conversion-focused layout, and mobile execution that holds up on a phone in daylight.

## Evidence on Hand

**None.** This is the site's central constraint.

There are no project photos, no testimonials, no client logos, no certifications, no years-in-business figure, and no project count. The build must not fabricate any of them.

Placeholder imagery is authorized for this build, on the condition that every placeholder is obviously a placeholder in the deliverable and is accompanied by a written asset request specifying exactly what photograph the client must supply for that slot. User-confirmed: correctly-sized, visibly-marked placeholder blocks labelled in BM with the exact shot required, plus a written client asset request list shipped with the build. No stock photography standing in for the client's own work. Trust must be constructed from things that are true — specialist focus, clear explanation of the method, site-condition-based approach, and easy direct contact — not from invented proof.

## Product Principles

1. **WhatsApp is the product's front door.** Every screen keeps contact within reach; nothing routes a ready visitor through an unnecessary form.
2. **Explain before selling.** A visitor who does not know what a gabion wall is cannot buy one. Education is a conversion mechanism, not filler.
3. **Speak in symptoms, not specifications.** Lead with the customer's observed problem ("tebing runtuh") and connect it to the solution — never open with technical vocabulary.
4. **Truth over polish.** No invented numbers, awards, history, or proof. Where evidence is missing, the design earns credibility through clarity and specificity instead of filling the gap with claims.
5. **Mobile on bad signal is the real condition.** Weight, image payload, and tap-target reality are design constraints, not post-launch optimizations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Non-technical, mixed-age, mobile-first audience. Requires generous type sizing, high text contrast against earth-tone backgrounds, tap targets comfortable for outdoor one-handed use, and no reliance on hover to reveal information.
