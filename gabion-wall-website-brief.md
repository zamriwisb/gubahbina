# Gabion Wall Website Build Brief

## Project objective
Build a professional lead-generation website in **Bahasa Melayu** for a Malaysian gabion wall specialist. The website should position the business as a specialist contractor for gabion wall supply and installation, focused on slope protection, erosion control, retaining structures, and site reinforcement.[cite:2][cite:7][cite:15]

The primary goal is to generate WhatsApp inquiries and quote requests from homeowners, developers, contractors, landowners, and businesses that need gabion wall solutions.[cite:7]

## Brand positioning
Use this core positioning:

**Pakar Memasang Gabion Wall untuk Tebing, Cerun & Kawalan Hakisan**

The brand should feel practical, trustworthy, engineering-focused, and locally relevant. Avoid a generic corporate or SaaS visual style. The website should reflect construction credibility, site work, and problem-solving for issues like hakisan, banjir, tanah runtuh, and tembok lemah.[cite:2][cite:4][cite:7][cite:15]

## Known source information
Use the following information as approved source material from the client and public business presence:

- Brand/service theme: **Pakar memasang Gabion Wall**.[cite:2][cite:4]
- Stated functions of gabion wall: menguatkan dinding atau tembok, menjadi asas empangan, mencegah hakisan, mencegah banjir, dan mencegah tanah runtuh.[cite:2][cite:4]
- Gabion systems are commonly used for retaining walls, erosion control, riverbank protection, slope stabilization, and drainage-friendly structural protection.[cite:7][cite:15]
- Gabions are permeable structures that help relieve water pressure and are often used where flexibility and drainage are important.[cite:7][cite:15]

Do not invent certifications, years of experience, government approvals, client logos, or project statistics unless supplied later by the client.

## Target audience
Primary audiences:

- Pemilik rumah yang ada masalah hakisan atau cerun.
- Pemilik tanah dan kebun.
- Pemaju perumahan dan kontraktor kecil.
- Kontraktor awam dan syarikat pembinaan.
- Pihak yang perlukan perlindungan tebing, longkang, sungai, atau kawasan cerun.[cite:7][cite:15]

## Core user problems
The messaging should address these common customer concerns:

- Tebing runtuh atau semakin terhakis.
- Kawasan rumah atau tapak terdedah kepada aliran air.
- Dinding penahan sedia ada tidak kukuh.
- Cerun berisiko bergerak atau runtuh.
- Perlukan sistem yang tahan lasak dan membenarkan saliran air.[cite:2][cite:7][cite:15]

## Primary calls to action
Use clear action-focused CTAs throughout the site:

- Dapatkan Sebut Harga
- WhatsApp Sekarang
- Minta Semakan Tapak
- Hubungi Pakar Gabion Wall
- Tanya Kos Pemasangan

Primary conversion should prioritize WhatsApp contact.

## Recommended sitemap
Create the website with these main pages/sections:

| Page | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Home | Introduce the service, explain gabion wall benefits, show trust signals, and drive inquiries. |
| Perkhidmatan | Detail installation services, retaining applications, erosion control, slope protection, and related works.[cite:7][cite:15] |

If a one-page website is preferred, keep the same information architecture as homepage sections.

## Homepage structure
Build the homepage in this order:

### 1. Hero section
Include:
- Strong headline in BM.
- Short supporting text.
- Primary WhatsApp CTA.
- Secondary CTA for quote or site inspection.
- Real project image or gabion installation image.

Suggested headline:

**Pakar Memasang Gabion Wall untuk Kawalan Hakisan, Cerun & Tebing**

Suggested supporting copy:

Menyediakan pemasangan gabion wall untuk menguatkan struktur tanah, membantu kawalan hakisan, mengurangkan risiko banjir, dan melindungi kawasan cerun atau tebing.[cite:2][cite:7][cite:15]

### 2. Problem section
Highlight real pain points such as:
- Hakisan tebing.
- Risiko tanah runtuh.
- Tembok tidak stabil.
- Aliran air yang merosakkan kawasan tapak.

Use simple, problem-led copy aimed at local customers.

### 3. What is gabion wall
Explain briefly what gabion wall is in BM. Keep it practical, not academic.

Suggested direction:
Gabion wall ialah struktur sangkar besi atau mesh yang diisi batu untuk menguatkan tanah, melindungi tebing, dan mengawal hakisan di kawasan tertentu.[cite:7][cite:15]

### 4. Fungsi / benefits section
Present the client's functions in polished BM:

- Menguatkan dinding atau tembok penahan.[cite:2][cite:7]
- Membantu sebagai sokongan struktur di kawasan tertentu.[cite:2][cite:7]
- Mencegah hakisan tanah dan tebing.[cite:2][cite:7][cite:15]
- Mengurangkan kesan aliran air dan membantu kawalan banjir di lokasi tertentu.[cite:2][cite:7][cite:15]
- Membantu mencegah risiko tanah runtuh.[cite:2][cite:7]

Also include modern practical benefits:
- Sistem lebih mesra saliran kerana air boleh mengalir keluar.[cite:7][cite:15]
- Struktur fleksibel untuk keadaan tanah tertentu.[cite:7]
- Kemasan batu nampak lebih natural dan tahan lasak.[cite:7]

### 5. Services section
Include cards or content blocks for:
- Pemasangan Gabion Wall
- Perlindungan Tebing
- Kawalan Hakisan
- Pengukuhan Cerun
- Tembok Penahan Batu
- Kerja berkaitan saliran atau pengukuhan tapak

Only include services that the client actually offers or can support.

### 6. Why choose us
Use non-fabricated trust-based reasons such as:
- Fokus khusus pada pemasangan gabion wall.
- Penyelesaian berdasarkan keadaan tapak.

## Copywriting style
The website copy should be:
- In **Bahasa Melayu** as the main language.
- Direct, practical, and easy to understand.
- Problem-solution oriented.
- Trustworthy and not exaggerated.
- Local in tone, suitable for the Malaysian market.

Avoid overusing technical jargon unless explained simply.

## Visual direction
Use a rugged, grounded, engineering-style visual system.

Recommended style:
- Earth tones, stone, sand, olive, dark gray, off-white.
- Strong typography with solid hierarchy.
- Real construction/project images.
- Minimal decorative effects.
- Clean section spacing and strong CTA visibility.

Avoid:
- SaaS gradients.
- Futuristic glowing UI.
- Tech startup aesthetics.
- Generic stock business people imagery.
- Over-designed animations.

The design should feel like a specialist contractor website, not a software product.
